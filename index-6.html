Two ways to work

Hire for the work you need

Choose the right route. Company hiring happens off-platform after rEcruit connects both sides; personal hiring is managed on rEcruit from task agreement through delivery and payment.

↗

Hiring for company

For companies hiring community managers, moderators, community leads and others. rEcruit introduces both sides; you agree terms and manage work and payment outside rEcruit. The company pays rEcruit a 2.5% platform fee based on the agreed contract value.

2.5% · Company pays rEcruit
✓

Hiring for myself

Hire someone to complete a specific task on rEcruit. Agree the task, give instructions, review delivery and pay through rEcruit. The 5% platform fee is added to the agreed job price, so the worker receives the full agreed amount.

5% · Added to hirer checkout

Post an opportunity

Enter the worker/contract agreement value to preview the fee. Company hiring: the 2.5% platform fee is payable by the company separately, while work and payment remain off-platform. Hiring for myself: the 5% fee is added at checkout so the worker receives the full agreed job price. Any payment-processing fee must be disclosed separately before checkout. Live payment collection is not connected in this demo.

How rEcruit works

Beyond CVs. Built on Reputation.

Included in this MVP

Responsive expert discovery, two hiring routes, opportunity posting, local demo messaging, 50-point starting reputation, evidence-backed Vouch/Refute review queue, profile creation and moderation simulation. Production still needs accounts, secure file storage, database, real-time messaging, payments/escrow and reviewer permissions.
